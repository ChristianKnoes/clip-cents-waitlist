Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Clip Cents ("we", "us") edits short-form video clips for businesses and creators and, with their permission, uploads those clips to their social media accounts. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Content you send us — videos, audio, images and notes you share so we can produce clips.
  • Contact details — your name, email address and any messages you send us.
  • Connected account data — when you connect a TikTok (or other social media) account through the platform's official login, we receive an access token, your account's unique ID, and basic profile information such as your display name and avatar. We never see or store your password.

How we use it

  • To edit your content into clips.
  • To upload finished clips to the accounts you have connected, only as you have authorised. On TikTok, clips are sent to your account as drafts that you review and publish yourself.
  • To communicate with you about your clips and our service.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the platforms you ask us to post to and the service providers we rely on to operate (such as cloud file storage).

TikTok data

Our use of TikTok is limited to uploading video content to the TikTok account you authorise, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. Access tokens are stored securely and used only for this purpose. You can revoke our access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, or by emailing us.

Retention and deletion

We keep your content and connected account data only as long as needed to provide our service. When you stop working with us, or at any time on request, we delete your access tokens and your content. Email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and restricted access. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Children

Our service is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
